Kongsberg Maritime Acquires Steerprop to Dominate Arctic Thruster Market

The Norwegian maritime giant secures a 400-vessel ice-class installed base through its strategic buyout of Finnish propulsion specialist Steerprop.

Published 8 Sept 2026 3 min read By Aditya

Kongsberg Maritime has agreed to acquire Finnish azimuth propulsion specialist Steerprop, strengthening its hold on the fast-growing Arctic and ice-class vessel segment.

In short

Kongsberg Maritime is acquiring Steerprop, a Finnish designer and manufacturer of azimuth propulsion systems, to strengthen its capabilities in Arctic and ice-class vessels. The deal brings together Steerprop's installed base of over 400 ice-class vessels with Kongsberg Maritime's global service network and systems integration scale.

Kongsberg Maritime has agreed to acquire Finnish azimuth propulsion specialist Steerprop to cement its competitive edge in polar and ice-class vessel segments, according to Container News. The acquisition integrates Steerprop’s portfolio of high-ice-class thrusters into Kongsberg Maritime’s broader systems integration network, global scale, and aftermarket lifecycle services. Steerprop brings an installed base exceeding 400 vessels worldwide alongside a robust order backlog dominated by demanding polar research ships, specialized icebreakers, and harsh-environment offshore support vessels.

Fleet renewal requirements and escalating maritime activity across extreme northern latitudes are accelerating demand for heavy-duty propulsion technology. Shipowners operating in these freezing operating environments face stringent class society requirements and operational risks that demand proven mechanical reliability. By combining Steerprop’s engineering heritage with Kongsberg Maritime’s extensive global service infrastructure, the merged entity aims to streamline delivery schedules and lifetime maintenance for specialized tonnage.

How does Steerprop's technology enhance Kongsberg's offerings?

Steerprop enhances Kongsberg Maritime's propulsion portfolio by supplying highly specialized azimuth thruster systems engineered specifically for high ice-class notations and extreme Arctic navigation. Steerprop has spent decades perfecting mechanical designs that withstand extreme ice-loading forces encountered by icebreakers and polar research vessels. Lisa Edvardsen Haugan, CEO of Kongsberg Maritime, noted that the acquisition represents a strategic maneuver within an expanding market segment, uniting complementary technical strengths. Ville Rimpilä, Senior Vice President for Thruster Systems at Kongsberg Maritime, emphasized that Steerprop’s established market leadership and client relationships will merge smoothly with the buyer's integrated solutions capability.

  • Steerprop brings a global installed base exceeding 400 vessels outfitted with azimuth propulsion.
  • The acquisition includes a substantial order backlog heavily weighted toward Arctic and ice-class projects.
  • Kongsberg Maritime integrates specialized Finnish engineering with its worldwide lifecycle services network.
  • Demand is propelled by mandatory fleet renewal programmes and rising geopolitical and commercial polar focus.

"This acquisition is a strategic step in a growing market segment, combining complementary strengths to create additional value for customers." — Lisa Edvardsen Haugan, CEO of Kongsberg Maritime

What drives demand for Arctic ice-class vessels?

Demand for Arctic ice-class vessels is driven by aging polar fleets, increased sovereign interest in northern maritime corridors, and expanded natural resource extraction in high-latitude zones. Shipowners and charterers require advanced propulsion architectures capable of continuous ice-breaking and dynamic positioning in sub-zero ambient temperatures. Shipyards building these specialized hulls demand robust azimuth units that satisfy strict class society structural rules while minimizing maintenance downtime during tight seasonal operational windows. This transaction positions the combined Norwegian and Finnish group to capture a larger share of upcoming orders for polar research vessels and offshore support craft.

What to watch next

Maritime stakeholders and naval architects should monitor three critical developments following this transaction:

First, observe regulatory clearances and antitrust approvals required to finalize the corporate acquisition within the European maritime engineering sector. Second, track how integration impacts delivery timelines for Steerprop’s existing order backlog across shipyards in Europe and Asia. Third, watch for upcoming joint product announcements that combine Kongsberg Maritime automation platforms with Steerprop’s heavy-duty mechanical thruster units.
