MB Shipbrokers Names Claes Devantier as New CEO in Planned Succession

Anders Hald to transition to chairman as the brokerage lines up its executive team for 2027.

Published 9 Sept 2026 2 min read By Marcus Singapore

MB Shipbrokers has announced a major leadership reshuffle, appointing Claes Devantier as CEO effective January 1, 2027, succeeding Anders Hald.

Key takeaways
  • Claes Devantier will assume the role of CEO at MB Shipbrokers starting January 1, 2027.
  • Outgoing CEO Anders Hald will transition to the position of chairman while staying commercially active.
  • Singapore-based Claus Plougmand has been appointed as deputy CEO of the brokerage firm.
  • The leadership reshuffle was officially reported by maritime publication Splash247 in September 2026.
In short

Claes Devantier has been named the next CEO of MB Shipbrokers, taking over from Anders Hald effective January 1, 2027. Anders Hald will move into the role of chairman, while Singapore-based Claus Plougmand will step in as deputy CEO as part of the planned leadership transition.

When leadership changes at a major brokerage house, charterers, shipowners, and operators watch closely for shifts in commercial strategy. Succession planning in maritime service providers directly impacts client relationships, desk execution, and long-term brokerage stability across global shipping hubs.

Who is the new MB Shipbrokers CEO?

Claes Devantier has been officially named as the next chief executive officer of MB Shipbrokers, stepping up to lead the established brokerage firm following a structured succession plan announced in September 2026. The appointment positions Devantier at the helm starting January 1, 2027, taking over the top executive duties from current chief executive Anders Hald. Devantier's deep familiarity with the firm's operations ensures continuity for clients relying on the house for dry cargo, tanker, or sale and purchase desks, maintaining the firm's competitive posture in fiercely contested commercial markets.

Broking houses require steady hands at the top to navigate volatile freight markets, shifting bunker prices, and complex charter party negotiations. By elevating internal talent with a proven track record, MB Shipbrokers aims to reassure principals and operators that day-to-day transaction execution will remain uninterrupted during the transition.

How is the executive team changing?

The executive reshuffle at MB Shipbrokers extends beyond the chief executive seat, establishing a restructured leadership tier that includes both executive continuity and regional strength in key maritime centers. Outgoing chief executive Anders Hald will not sever ties with the business, but will instead assume the role of chairman while maintaining an active commercial presence and close engagement with core clients. Additionally, Singapore-based Claus Plougmand has been appointed as deputy chief executive officer, reinforcing the firm's strategic commitment to Asian shipping markets and ensuring robust operational oversight across international time zones.

What to watch next

Tracking brokerage leadership transitions requires monitoring how regional desks and key broker movements respond to executive changes over the coming months. Stakeholders should keep an eye on three specific developments as the January 2027 effective date approaches:

  • Formal announcements regarding any structural desk reassignments under Claes Devantier's incoming administration.
  • The continued commercial visibility of Anders Hald in his new capacity as chairman.
  • Operational developments out of the Singapore office under deputy CEO Claus Plougmand to gauge regional growth.
