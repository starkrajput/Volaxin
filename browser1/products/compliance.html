Why one compliance engine

Six regulations. Six deadlines. Six penalty regimes. One dataset should power all of them.

Managing CII, ETS, FuelEU, MRV, and DCS as separate workflows means six versions of the same underlying voyage data — and inevitable divergence. VOLAX Comply runs all six from one operational feed, so what you report to IMO matches what you report to the EU verifier matches what you settle with the charterer.

Ten Capabilities

Regulatory alignment, done once

CII attained calculation

Attained annual efficiency ratio computed continuously. A–E rating with 1-, 3-, and 5-year projections. Correction factors applied per operational profile.

EU ETS & UK ETS allowance tracking

EUA and UKA allowance holdings, voyage-level emissions allocation, phase-in schedule, penalty estimation. Charterer contribution scenarios.

FuelEU Maritime GHG intensity

Well-to-wake GHG intensity per voyage. Reduction trajectory against 2025, 2030, 2035 targets. Penalty estimation. Alternative fuel scenarios.

Fleet pooling optimisation

Identify the lowest-cost combination of vessels for FuelEU pooling. Model outcomes before committing. Report pool submissions cleanly.

EU MRV & IMO DCS reports

Verified annual reports generated from operational data. Direct verifier upload where supported. Historical filings preserved for audit.

Decarbonisation pathway planning

Target-setting per vessel per year. Progress tracking. Alignment with Poseidon Principles reporting for lender disclosure.

Voyage-level emissions allocation

Emissions attributed per voyage, per cargo lot, per charterer. Direct feed to charterer scope-3 reporting.

Alternative fuel impact modelling

Model LNG, methanol, ammonia, biofuel blends. Impact on CII, ETS cost, FuelEU compliance, and voyage economics.

Regulatory change tracking

Track upcoming amendments (IMO MEPC, EU legislation, national schemes). Impact assessment on current fleet. Preparation timeline.

Automated data feed from VOLAX Ops

Noon reports and logbooks feed Comply automatically. No re-entry, no reconciliation, no divergent numbers.
