Why generic ERP fails at sea

A vessel is a profit centre, a cost centre, a moving asset, and a currency crossroads — all at once.

Generic ERPs treat every business unit as a static entity. A vessel is anything but: it changes port, currency, charter, and cost profile weekly. VOLAX Finance was built to model the ship as its own accounting dimension — every cost tagged, every voyage measured, every hire currency handled, every consolidation seamless.
