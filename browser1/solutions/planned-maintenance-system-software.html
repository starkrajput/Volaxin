Planned Maintenance System (PMS) Software

Volaxin's marine PMS software keeps every maintainable component on board scheduled, evidenced and audit-ready — calendar and running-hours jobs, ISM 10.3 critical equipment, defect reporting, linked spares, class-survey credits and dry-dock planning — all working fully offline on the vessel and syncing automatically to the office.

Class-approved schemes Offline at sea PSC & SIRE ready

Maintenance That Runs the Fleet — Not the Other Way Around

Unplanned machinery failure remains the single largest driver of off-hire, detentions and insurance claims in commercial shipping. Volaxin's planned maintenance system (PMS) software replaces spreadsheets and disconnected tools with one living database of every component, job, counter, spare and record — the vessel's institutional memory across crew changes and years of service.

Because the PMS shares its dataset with inventory, procurement and crewing, completing an overhaul decrements stock and raises a requisition automatically — no re-keying, no silo reconciliation.

Core Capabilities

Equipment Hierarchy & Technical Library

A structured tree — vessel → system → equipment → component — carrying makers' data, drawings, manuals and certificates. Everything in the PMS hangs off this hierarchy.

Job Scheduling & Work Orders

Calendar and counter-based jobs with instructions, safety precautions, required spares and tools. Running-hour counters from noon reports drive the due list the chief engineer actually works from.

Critical Equipment (ISM 10.3)

Critical items flagged and locked to stricter workflows, with standby-arrangement testing and reliability measures evidenced for audit.

Class & Survey Integration

CMS items mapped to class survey codes with windows, postponements and credits tracked — survey exposure visible across the fleet at a glance.

Defect & Dry-Dock Management

Breakdown reporting with cause analysis feeding corrective actions, plus long-horizon dry-dock planning that aggregates postponed jobs and owner's work into a costed specification.

Offline Ship–Shore Synchronisation

The vessel database works fully offline and reconciles automatically over low-bandwidth VSAT or Starlink — no duplicated records, no lost counter readings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PMS software on a ship?

A planned maintenance system that schedules, executes and documents maintenance by calendar, running hours or condition — keeping the vessel compliant with ISM paragraph 10 and class requirements.

Is Volaxin PMS class-approved?

Yes — it supports class-approved PMS schemes and continuous machinery survey (CMS) arrangements with survey-code mapping and credit tracking.

Does it work offline?

Yes — full offline operation on the vessel with automatic conflict-free sync to the office.

PMS vs CMMS — what's the difference?

A marine PMS is a CMMS built for ships: class integration, ISM 10.3 critical equipment, offline sync and PSC-ready reporting.
