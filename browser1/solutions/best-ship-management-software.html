The 2026 Landscape

Ship management software falls into three broad groups. Knowing which you are buying matters more than any feature checkbox:

  • Unified suites — one platform covering technical, crewing, commercial and compliance on a shared dataset. Fewer integration seams; one contract and training curve. Volaxin Maritime Suite sits here.
  • Established modular suites — mature, enterprise-focused platforms, often assembled module-by-module and configured per customer. Examples widely used across the industry include DNV ShipManager, IFS / SpecTec AMOS, ABS Nautical Systems (NS), SERTICA (Logimatic) and BASSnet.
  • Best-of-breed point solutions — specialist tools for a single domain (a standalone PMS, crewing system, or chartering package) that must be integrated with the rest of your stack.
⚖️

The named platforms above are all credible, long-established products. This guide is deliberately vendor-neutral on them: rather than rank other companies' tools with claims we can't verify for your fleet, it gives you the criteria to evaluate any of them — and Volaxin — objectively.

The Criteria That Actually Predict Success

Most selection processes over-weight the demo and under-weight the realities of running software across a fleet at sea. Score every vendor — including us — on these:

CriterionWhy it decides the outcomeQuestion to ask every vendor
Offline resilienceVessels lose connectivity; the system must keep working and reconcile cleanly."Show the vessel operating fully offline, then syncing with conflicts resolved — over low-bandwidth VSAT."
True integrationValue comes from PMS, spares, procurement, crewing and compliance sharing one dataset — not CSV exports."When a maintenance job is completed, does it decrement stock and raise a requisition automatically?"
Class & regulatory fitPMS scheme approvals, MLC/STCW rest-hour logic and CII/EU ETS/FuelEU reporting must be built in."Which class societies have approved your PMS scheme, and is EU ETS/FuelEU reporting native?"
On-board usabilityIf crews can't use it after a short handover, data quality collapses and so does the ROI."Can a relief second engineer complete a job on a tablet in the engine room without training?"
Data migrationThe system inherits the quality of the hierarchy, histories and counters you load."What is your structured migration methodology for our existing PMS and certificates?"
