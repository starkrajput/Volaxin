About Volaxin

Built by maritime professionals, for maritime professionals.

Volaxin was founded on a simple observation: the maritime industry runs on fragmented software. One system for maintenance, another for procurement, a third for crew, a fourth for chartering, and a spreadsheet to tie them all together. We set out to build the platform that should have existed from the beginning — one where every module shares the same data, offline operation is a founding principle, and AI assists rather than replaces maritime professionals.

Our Values

The principles that shape every release

Maritime First

Every feature is designed for how ships actually operate — a Filipino second engineer at a tablet at the machine, a superintendent in a Singapore office managing a fleet of forty, a broker in London finalising a fixture at 03:00 local. Our features begin with those people.

Integrity

We do not fabricate capabilities. We do not promise features we cannot deliver. Every claim on this website corresponds to code you can see in the platform. Every UNIQUE and RARE badge is validated against the actual competitive landscape.

Offline by Design

The vessel system is a complete working application, not a thin client. The moment your crew needs the software most is the moment satellite bandwidth is worst. We founded the platform on that reality.

Support you can hold us to

48-hour onboarding guarantee. 99.9% uptime SLA. Maritime-experienced support team — not a generic helpdesk. Direct-dial escalation to engineering when it matters.
