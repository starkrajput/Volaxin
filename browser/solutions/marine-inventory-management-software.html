Marine Inventory Management Software

Volaxin gives live, fleet-wide control of ship spares, stores and consumables — min/max levels, automated reorder, multi-warehouse and bonded stores, barcode stocktakes and consumption analytics — with every spare linked directly to the maintenance job that uses it and to procurement that replenishes it.

Stop Guessing What's On Board

Overstocking ties up capital in slow-moving spares; understocking a critical part causes off-hire. Volaxin's marine inventory management software ends the guesswork with a live picture of every item across every vessel and warehouse, and the analytics to right-size stock over time.

The key is integration: spares are linked to PMS jobs and to procurement, so consumption, reorder and purchasing move as one flow rather than three disconnected spreadsheets.

Core Capabilities

  • Live fleet-wide visibility — real-time stock by vessel, store location and shore warehouse, including bonded stores.
  • Min/max & automated reorder — threshold-driven reorder proposals that become requisitions in one click.
  • Spare-to-job linkage — completing a maintenance job decrements the exact spares used and flags replenishment.
  • Multi-warehouse & transfers — inter-vessel and vessel-to-shore transfers with full traceability.
  • Barcode / QR stocktakes — fast, accurate physical counts reconciled to the system.
  • Consumption & cost analytics — usage trends, slow-moving stock and cost per running hour.
  • Offline at sea — stock movements recorded without connectivity and synced automatically.

The Payoff

  • Lower working capital — right-sized stock instead of dead inventory.
  • Fewer breakdowns — critical spares are on board when the job is due.
  • Faster purchasing — reorder proposals flow straight into procurement.
  • Clean audits — traceable movements and accurate counts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is marine inventory management software?

Software that tracks ship spares, stores and consumables fleet-wide with live levels, automated reorder, multi-warehouse support and consumption analytics — linked to the PMS.

Does it link spares to maintenance jobs?

Yes — completing a PMS job decrements the exact spares used and triggers reorder.

Can it handle multiple vessels and warehouses?

Yes — including shore and bonded stores, with offline stock movements at sea.
