Crew Management Software

Volaxin crew management software runs the entire seafarer lifecycle from one system — certificate and STCW expiry alerts, rotation and relief planning, payroll and allotments, travel, appraisals and P&I compliance — sharing one crew list with work & rest hours and the vessel's PMS so nobody sails with an expired document and no relief is planned late.

STCW & certificate alerts Payroll & allotments MLC-linked

The Whole Seafarer Lifecycle, One System

Crewing failures are expensive and public: an expired certificate found at Port State Control is a detention; a botched relief is a flight, a hotel and an overrun contract. Volaxin's crew management software replaces spreadsheets and email with a single fleet-wide crew list where every certificate, contract, rotation and payment is tracked and alerted in advance.

Because crewing shares data with work & rest hours and PMS, the same person record drives manning, MLC compliance and the maintenance workload on board.

Core Capabilities

Certificates & STCW Compliance

Every certificate, endorsement, visa and medical tracked with automated expiry alerts to crewing and the vessel — no seafarer sails out of certification.

Rotation & Relief Planning

Sign-on/sign-off planning with contract lengths, reliever availability, rank matching and travel lead-times, so reliefs are visible months ahead.

Payroll & Allotments

Wage scales by rank and flag, overtime, allotments and multi-currency payments, with payslips and full audit trail.

Travel & Logistics

Crew-change travel, documentation and cost tracking tied to the rotation plan.

Appraisals & Records

Performance appraisals, training records, sea-time and a complete history that follows the seafarer across contracts and vessels.

Flag-State & P&I Compliance

Manning against flag-state requirements and Maritime Labour Convention obligations, with the evidence PSC and P&I expect.

Why It Matters

  • No expired documents at sea — advance alerts prevent detentions and off-hire.
  • Planned reliefs, lower cost — fewer emergency crew changes and travel overruns.
  • One record, everywhere — crewing, rest hours and maintenance share the same seafarer data.
  • Audit-ready MLC — rest-hour and manning evidence produced as a by-product of daily work.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does crew management software do?

It manages the seafarer lifecycle fleet-wide: certificates and STCW, rotation and relief, payroll and allotments, travel, appraisals, and flag-state / P&I compliance.

Does it track certificate expiry?

Yes — every certificate and medical is tracked with automated advance alerts.

Is it linked to rest hours?

Yes — it shares the crew list with the Work & Rest Hours module for MLC 2006 compliance.
