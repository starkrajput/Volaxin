Vessel Tracking & Voyage Planning Software

Volaxin brings live vessel tracking, passage planning, weather routing, speed-versus-fuel optimisation, port congestion alerts and dynamic ETA into one view — and feeds every voyage's fuel and distance data straight into CII and MRV reporting, so navigation decisions and compliance stay joined up.

Live fleet tracking Weather routing CII-linked

Every Voyage, Optimised and Visible

Fuel is the largest controllable cost at sea and, increasingly, the largest compliance exposure. Volaxin's voyage planning and vessel tracking software gives the office a live picture of the fleet and gives navigators the tools to plan the safest, most efficient passage — then turns the resulting data into the CII and MRV evidence regulators require.

Core Capabilities

  • Live vessel tracking — real-time positions, headings and status across the fleet on one map.
  • Passage & route planning — waypoints, ETAs and route libraries per trade.
  • Weather routing — forecast-aware route options that protect schedule, fuel and safety.
  • Speed vs fuel optimisation — leg-by-leg trade-offs against charter-party speed and ETA.
  • Port congestion & ETA — dynamic ETA management with congestion and waiting-time signals.
  • Reporting integration — noon and voyage reports feeding CII, MRV/DCS and performance analytics.

The Payoff

  • Lower fuel spend — optimised speed and routing on every leg.
  • Better CII ratings — efficiency decisions feed directly into carbon compliance.
  • Schedule reliability — dynamic ETA that reflects weather and congestion.
  • One source of voyage truth — operations, commercial and compliance on the same data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is voyage planning software?

Software to plan and optimise a ship's passage — route, weather routing, speed/fuel and ETA — with live tracking and voyage performance data for the office.

Does it include weather routing?

Yes — forecast-aware routing with speed-versus-fuel optimisation per leg.

Is voyage data linked to CII?

Yes — fuel and distance feed IMO CII and EU MRV / IMO DCS reporting automatically.
