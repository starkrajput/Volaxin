Maritime Procurement Software

Volaxin runs ship purchasing from requisition to delivery — RFQ and side-by-side supplier bid comparison, purchase-order approval workflows, budget and spend analytics, and three-way invoice matching — with requisitions flowing straight from inventory reorder and PMS jobs so you buy what the fleet actually needs.

RFQ & bid comparison Budget control Inventory-linked

Purchasing Without the Paper Chase

Ship purchasing spans dozens of chandlers and OEMs, multiple currencies and constant budget pressure. Volaxin's maritime procurement software replaces email RFQs and spreadsheet comparisons with a controlled requisition-to-delivery workflow that is faster, cheaper and fully auditable.

Because requisitions originate from inventory reorder points and PMS jobs, purchasing reflects real consumption — not guesswork — and received goods update stock automatically.

Core Capabilities

  • Requisition-to-delivery workflow — vessel or office requisitions routed through configurable approvals.
  • RFQ & supplier bid comparison — quotes compared side by side on price, lead time and terms.
  • Purchase orders & approvals — multi-level authorisation with spend limits by role.
  • Budget & spend analytics — live budget vs actual by vessel, category and supplier.
  • Invoice matching — three-way match of PO, goods received and invoice.
  • Supplier & catalogue management — supplier performance, catalogues and framework pricing.

The Payoff

  • Lower unit cost — competitive quotes on every order instead of default suppliers.
  • Shorter cycle time — requisition to PO in hours, not days.
  • Budget discipline — spend visible and controlled before it happens.
  • Closed loop — purchasing, inventory and maintenance on one dataset.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is maritime procurement software?

Software that manages ship purchasing end to end — requisition, RFQ, supplier comparison, PO approval, delivery and invoice matching — with budget analytics and inventory/PMS integration.

Does it compare supplier quotes?

Yes — RFQs go to multiple suppliers and quotes are compared side by side with a full audit trail.

Is it linked to inventory and maintenance?

Yes — requisitions originate from reorder thresholds and PMS jobs, and receipts update stock automatically.
