Volaxin records seafarers' hours of work and rest and checks them automatically against both MLC 2006 and STCW limits — flagging violations in real time, planning rest before breaches happen, and producing the PSC-ready records inspectors ask for — all on the same crew list as crewing and payroll.

Rest-Hour Compliance Without the Guesswork

Rest-hour breaches are a leading Port State Control deficiency and a fatigue-driven safety risk. Volaxin's work & rest hours software takes the ambiguity out of it: enter hours once and the system validates them against both regulatory regimes at the same time, warns before a limit is breached, and keeps the audit trail intact.

Core Capabilities

  • Dual-regime checking — MLC 2006 and STCW rest requirements validated simultaneously.
  • Automatic violation detection — breaches flagged the moment hours are recorded.
  • Real-time rest planning — see exposure ahead and plan watches to stay compliant.
  • PSC-ready export — inspector-format records and reports on demand.
  • Non-conformity workflow — violations linked to corrective action and SHEQ evidence.
  • Crew-list integration — the same seafarer record as crew management.

The Payoff

  • Fewer PSC deficiencies — breaches caught and corrected before inspection.
  • Lower fatigue risk — a genuine safety and SHEQ improvement.
  • Less admin — enter once, validate automatically, export in seconds.
