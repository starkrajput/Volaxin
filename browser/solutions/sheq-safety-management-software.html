SHEQ & Marine Safety Management Software

Volaxin's SHEQ software runs your safety management system end to end — incidents and near-misses, audits and inspections, risk assessments, drills, corrective actions and KPIs — aligned to the ISM Code, ISPS and ISO, with a controlled SMS document library and the vetting evidence SIRE 2.0 and RightShip expect.

ISM / ISPS / ISO Vetting-ready CAPA workflow

One Safety System, Fleet-Wide

Safety performance is now a commercial gatekeeper: charterers screen on SIRE 2.0 and RightShip before a fixture. Volaxin's marine safety management (SHEQ) software turns scattered forms and binders into one auditable system where every incident, audit finding and non-conformity is tracked to closure and every document is the controlled current version.

Core Capabilities

  • Incident & near-miss reporting — structured capture with root-cause analysis and trending.
  • Audits & inspections — internal, external, PSC and vetting with findings tracked to closure.
  • Risk assessment — permits to work and risk assessments tied to operations.
  • Drills & training — scheduling, records and evidence of shipboard drills.
  • Corrective & preventive actions (CAPA) — one workflow fed by maintenance, rest-hours and inspection non-conformities.
  • SMS document control — controlled, versioned safety-management-system library with offline vessel sync.
  • Safety KPIs — LTIF, near-miss ratios and audit closure across the fleet.

The Payoff

  • Better vetting outcomes — evidence ready for SIRE 2.0 and RightShip.
  • Fewer repeat findings — CAPA that actually closes the loop.
  • Stronger safety culture — near-miss reporting that people use.
  • Connected, not siloed — safety linked to how the fleet operates.
