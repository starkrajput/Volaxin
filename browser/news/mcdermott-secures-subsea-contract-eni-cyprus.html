McDermott Secures Major Subsea Contract for Eni Cyprus Project

Houston-based contractor lands substantial deal for the fast-tracked Cronos deepwater gas development.

Published 8 Sept 2026 2 min read By Marcus Houston

McDermott secures a major subsea contract worth up to $750m from Eni for the fast-tracked Cronos gas development offshore Cyprus.

Key takeaways
  • McDermott secured a subsea contract valued between $500m and $750m from energy major Eni.
  • The contract covers the fast-tracked Cronos gas development located offshore Cyprus.
  • The Houston-based contractor will handle project management, engineering, procurement, and installation.
  • McDermott classifies the significant deepwater award within its substantial project tier.
In short

McDermott has secured a subsea contract valued between $500m and $750m from Eni for the fast-tracked Cronos gas development offshore Cyprus. The Houston-based contractor will provide project management, engineering, procurement, and installation support for the deepwater project.

Project Overview and Contract Value

Houston-based offshore contractor McDermott has secured a high-value subsea contract valued between $500m and $750m from energy major Eni to support the fast-tracked Cronos gas development located offshore Cyprus in the Eastern Mediterranean. The substantial agreement bolsters McDermott's growing orderbook with critical deepwater engineering scope, encompassing full project management, detailed engineering, procurement, and installation support. By classifying this significant award within its defined substantial tier, McDermott reinforces its ongoing operational footprint in complex regional gas infrastructure projects, collaborating closely with Eni to accelerate offshore production timelines for the Cronos field discovery.

The fast-tracked nature of the Cronos development places strict demands on subsea architecture and supply chain coordination. Operators across the Eastern Mediterranean are increasingly prioritizing rapid monetization of recent gas discoveries to meet European energy security demands. This commercial urgency requires engineering contractors to mobilize specialized offshore vessels and deepwater construction spreads rapidly, minimizing bottlenecks between the initial front-end engineering design phase and final subsea installation.

Scope of Work and Engineering Execution

McDermott will execute comprehensive project management, engineering, procurement, and installation (EPCI) services for the deepwater subsea architecture tied to the Eni-operated Cronos gas development offshore Cyprus. The Houston contractor’s global project management teams and specialized engineering hubs will oversee the fabrication and deployment of subsea production systems, umbilical lines, and rigid flowlines designed to withstand harsh deepwater operating pressures and seabed conditions. Executing a fast-tracked EPCI scope of this magnitude demands meticulous coordination across multiple international fabrication yards, specialized offshore construction vessels, and local regulatory bodies in Cyprus.

  • Provision of end-to-end project management and detailed subsea engineering
  • Procurement of specialized subsea hardware, flowlines, and control umbilicals
  • Installation support utilizing advanced offshore construction and pipelay vessels
  • Integration with existing regional processing infrastructure and floating production units
"The substantial contract win reinforces McDermott's proven capability to deliver complex deepwater subsea infrastructure under aggressive project schedules."

What to watch next

Industry stakeholders and maritime logistics providers monitoring the Eastern Mediterranean offshore sector should track three critical milestones as the Eni and McDermott partnership advances the Cronos development. First, watch for announcements regarding the chartering of specialized heavy-lift and pipelay vessels required for the deepwater installation phase offshore Cyprus. Second, monitor local port infrastructure developments in Limassol or Larnaca as they scale up to support offshore supply base operations and subsea staging. Third, observe any updates from Eni regarding downstream regulatory approvals and gas export routing agreements that could further accelerate the fast-tracked production timeline.
