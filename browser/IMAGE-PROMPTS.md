# Volaxin Website — Image Prompt Manifest

Every image on the Standard site. Generate as `.webp` at the paths listed below. Style is consistent across the entire site: photo-realistic maritime, with a subtle blue-violet-gold Volaxin colour cast baked in. No stock-photo look.

## Global style guide (paste at top of every prompt)

> Photo-realistic maritime image. Cinematic lighting, high dynamic range. Colour grading with cool sapphire blues, soft violet highlights, and warm gold accents from equipment lights or golden-hour sun. Sharp mid-ground focus, subtle depth blur at edges. Composition suitable for a wide 4:3 or 16:9 web hero. No visible logos, no text, no watermarks. Ultra-crisp detail, professional maritime photography.

## Generator notes

- Prefer photo-realism over stylised illustration.
- Never render text; keep any dashboard imagery abstract and non-legible.
- Consistent maritime golden-hour palette across the site — this is the visual signature.
- No competitor branding, no real ship names, no crew member likenesses recognisable.
- All images to be optimised as `.webp` at 85 quality after generation (target ≤ 250KB each).
