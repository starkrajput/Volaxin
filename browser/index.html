Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you ask

Platform
What is Volaxin Maritime Suite?
Volaxin Maritime Suite is a cloud-based ship management software platform with 11 integrated modules including PMS, Crew Management, Chartering, Navigation & Voyage Planning, Inventory, Procurement, Work Rest Hours, Vessel Tracking, and Live Weather Intelligence.
Connectivity
Does Volaxin support offline ship-side operations?
Yes. Volaxin supports seamless online and offline cloud sync, allowing vessel crews to operate without internet connectivity and synchronise data when connection is restored.
Compliance
Is Volaxin MLC 2006 compliant?
Yes. Volaxin's Work & Rest Hours module is fully MLC 2006 and STCW compliant with automated violation detection and PSC-ready export.
Chartering
Does Volaxin include chartering and post-fixture management?
Yes. The Chartering module covers charter party and fixture management, voyage estimation and P&L, laytime and demurrage calculations, freight, hire and commission invoicing, and bunker and port cost tracking — fully integrated with operations and reporting.
Navigation
Can Volaxin plan and optimise voyages?
Yes. The Navigation module provides passage planning, weather-routing AI, speed versus fuel optimisation, dynamic ETA management, port congestion alerts, and real-time vessel tracking.
Scale
How many vessels does Volaxin manage?
Volaxin currently manages 500+ vessels across 60+ client companies and 15+ flag states with a 99.9% platform uptime SLA.
