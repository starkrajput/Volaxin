Why hull data belongs in the platform

A UT reading taken today is worthless in a PDF on a superintendent’s laptop next year.

Hull integrity is a decade-long trending story. Point-in-time surveys captured in isolated reports lose their trending power the moment they leave the surveyor’s hands. VOLAX Hull captures the reading in-place, plots it against history at that exact location, and surfaces the accelerating corrosion at that specific transverse frame before it becomes a Condition of Class.

Ten Capabilities

Structural intelligence, compartment by compartment

Compartment-level inspection

Every compartment (cargo hold, ballast tank, void space, cofferdam, engine room) modelled with its structural elements. Observation recording per element, per inspection.

Coating condition assessment

Coating breakdown percentage per compartment, per area. Deterioration trending across surveys. Re-coating projected against yard planning windows.

Corrosion monitoring

Corrosion location history at named structural points. Cyclical loading and cathodic protection effectiveness tracked. Critical structural areas flagged.

UT thickness recording & trending

Ultrasonic thickness measurements captured against structural element specification. Trend charts per measurement point. Class-diminution limits enforced.

Risk matrices with criticality

Each finding assigned criticality and risk code. Fleet-level risk map. Structural risk reporting for owner and class.

Equipment tracking

Anodes (sacrificial and ICCP), pipes, valves, ladders, heating coils. Location, condition, replacement cycle, and remaining life per component.

Steel weight estimation

Renewal steel weight and surface area calculated from inspection findings — feeding yard tender in VOLAX DryDock with accurate scope estimates.

Fleet & sister vessel comparison

Compare structural condition across sister vessels. Identify design-related patterns. Inform newbuild specifications.

Photo documentation with annotation

In-situ photos with annotation tools. Finding cross-reference. Time-stamped evidence for class discussion and warranty support.

Class survey integration

Class survey items and Conditions of Class tracked through the platform. COC-to-repair-to-close linkage preserved for the vessel life.
