Ten Capabilities

Nine sub-modules, one safety culture

Incident & near-miss reporting

Severity classification, immediate actions, investigation team assignment, root-cause analysis (5-Why, fishbone), corrective and preventive actions with owner and due date. Fleet-wide trend visibility.

Risk assessment

Hazard identification, likelihood × severity matrices per activity, control measures with residual risk scoring. Task-specific and generic risk assessments preserved with review cycles.

Permit-to-Work

Digital PTW for hot work, enclosed space entry, working aloft, cold work, isolation. Checklists, signatures, gas readings captured in-line. Automatic expiry with re-issue tracking.

Audit & inspection management

Internal audit planning, execution, finding logging, corrective action tracking, close-out verification. External audit history (Class, Flag, PSC, OMV) with themed trend analysis.

TMSA compliance

All twelve elements mapped to evidence artefacts across the platform. Self-assessment scoring, gap analysis, and improvement tracking. TMSA 3 stage-progression roadmap.

SIRE 2.0 vetting preparation

PIQ, CVIQ, and 12-chapter question library pre-populated from operational data. Photo evidence tied to inspector observations. HVPQ synchronised.

Management of Change

Change proposal, impact assessment across departments, approval workflow, implementation tracking, post-implementation review. MoC log for audit review.

Training & drills

Regulatory drill calendar, performance analysis, learning distribution. Emergency drill outcomes fed to lessons-learned. Familiarisation for new sign-ons tracked to competence matrix.

Lessons learned distribution

Bulletin publication with fleet-wide acknowledgment tracking per rank. Cross-reference to originating incident, related risk assessments, and corrective actions.

Regulatory documentation

ISM SMS, ISPS SSP, SOLAS FSA, MARPOL SOPEP/SMPEP, PSC preparation folders. Version-controlled through VOLAX Docs, distributed offline to every vessel.
