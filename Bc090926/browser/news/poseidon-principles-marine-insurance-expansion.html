Poseidon Principles Expands Reach Across Marine Insurance

Marine insurers and P&I clubs can now join the climate alignment framework as associate members following a unanimous vote.

Published 9 Sept 2026 3 min read By Aditya

The Poseidon Principles have expanded associate membership to include marine insurers and P&I clubs, aligning hull and machinery underwriting with shipping decarbonisation targets.

Key takeaways
  • Poseidon Principles associate membership has officially expanded to include marine insurers and P&I clubs.
  • The expansion follows a unanimous vote by existing signatories during their annual meeting in May.
  • Signatories to the Poseidon Principles currently represent nearly three-quarters of global shipping finance.
  • Underwriters can now track and disclose the climate alignment of their insured fleets using standardized metrics.
In short

The Poseidon Principles have expanded their associate membership to include marine insurers and P&I clubs. This allows risk underwriters to adopt the same climate alignment and greenhouse gas accounting methodologies used by shipping finance banks.

  1. How do marine insurers adopt the Poseidon Principles framework?
  2. Commercial consequences for shipowners and operators
  3. What to watch next

When capital providers start measuring their climate impact, the entire commercial shipping ecosystem eventually has to follow. The Poseidon Principles framework has officially opened its doors to marine insurers and P&I clubs, bridging the gap between maritime finance and underwriting risk.

This strategic expansion means underwriters can now adopt the same standardized greenhouse gas accounting methodologies that currently govern nearly three-quarters of global ship finance. Fleet managers, charterers, and shipowners who have spent recent years reporting their carbon intensity metrics to lending banks must now prepare for parallel transparency requirements from their hull and machinery underwriters and protection and indemnity mutuals.

How do marine insurers adopt the Poseidon Principles framework?

Marine insurers implement the Poseidon Principles by voluntarily aligning their underwriting portfolios with international climate goals, specifically measuring and disclosing the carbon intensity of insured fleets against established decarbonisation trajectories. Participating underwriters must calculate the annual carbon emissions of their covered vessels using verified operational data, comparing actual performance against decarbonisation trajectories mandated by international maritime regulators. This process relies on the same data-sharing mechanisms already established for greenhouse gas reporting under existing maritime compliance structures. By bringing marine insurers and P&I clubs into the fold, the initiative creates a unified front where both capital lending and risk underwriting depend on verifiable environmental performance. Underwriters gain a standardized methodology to evaluate transition risk across their books of business, while shipowners face a more integrated commercial environment where green vessels potentially secure better terms across both credit lines and insurance premiums.

  • Signatories represent nearly three-quarters of global shipping finance, giving the initiative massive commercial leverage.
  • A unanimous vote by existing signatories at the annual meeting in May authorized the inclusion of insurers and related organizations.
  • P&I clubs and hull underwriters can now join as associate members to track and disclose the climate alignment of their portfolios.
"The inclusion of marine insurers and P&I clubs bridges the crucial gap between maritime finance and risk underwriting, setting a unified decarbonisation standard across the industry."

Commercial consequences for shipowners and operators

Commercial operators must recognize that this underwriting expansion transforms climate disclosure from a banking requirement into a universal maritime license to operate. Shipowners who have optimized their vessels to satisfy financial lenders under existing Poseidon Principles metrics will find their efforts now directly influence their insurance costs and risk profiles. Conversely, asset owners operating older, carbon-intensive tonnage may face a tightening market where both credit availability and insurance capacity simultaneously contract. Class societies and port operators will likely see increased demand for verified CII and EEXI data as underwriters demand granular emissions reporting before writing coverage. This convergence of finance and insurance creates a powerful commercial incentive for fleet renewal, energy-saving device retrofits, and operational efficiency measures.

What to watch next

Industry stakeholders monitoring this maritime insurance expansion should track three critical milestones over the coming months:

  • The formal release of specific reporting guidelines and carbon trajectory baselines tailored for hull and machinery underwriters.
  • The announcement of pioneer P&I clubs and marine insurance houses officially signing up as the first associate members under the new rules.
  • Feedback from shipowners regarding potential shifts in insurance premiums, deductible structures, or underwriting capacity for non-compliant vessels.

Frequently asked

What are the Poseidon Principles?

The Poseidon Principles are a framework used by financial institutions to track and publicly disclose the climate alignment of their ship finance portfolios against international decarbonisation goals.

Who is eligible to join the Poseidon Principles now?

Following a unanimous vote by signatories, associate membership has expanded to include marine insurers, P&I clubs, and related maritime risk organizations alongside traditional shipping banks.

How will marine insurers use the Poseidon Principles?

Marine insurers will voluntarily measure and disclose the carbon intensity of their insured vessel portfolios, comparing actual vessel emissions against established decarbonisation trajectories.

What proportion of shipping finance do Poseidon Principles signatories represent?

Signatories to the Poseidon Principles represent nearly three-quarters of the global shipping finance portfolio, giving the initiative substantial commercial leverage across maritime trade.
