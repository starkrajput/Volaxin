Modules That Matter Most

The bulk-critical modules on the Volaxin platform

VOLAX Hull

Cargo hold coating tracking, tank-top inspections, cross-deck strength assessment. UT trending at load points. Structural condition per hold and per frame.

VOLAX PMS

Hatch cover maintenance (chalk test, hose test, ultrasonic tightness), deck crane servicing, grab / conveyor upkeep, cargo hold cleaning procedures.

VOLAX ChartExec

Voyage estimation for multi-port bulk trades. Laytime calculation across load and discharge terms. Reversible laytime, notices of readiness, waiting-time attribution.

VOLAX SHEQ

Enclosed-space entry permits, IMSBC-code cargo hazard assessments, fumigation safety procedures, hot-work in cargo hold permits.

VOLAX Ops

Cargo record book, ballast operations logging, cargo hold sequence and stability calculations, draught survey capture.

VOLAX Inventory

Hold-cleaning consumables, mucking spares, deck crane hydraulic parts, hatch cover rubber seals. Ready before the next fixture.
