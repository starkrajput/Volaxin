Why cross-module BI matters

The rising defect rate on ME.02 relates to the delayed spare on PO#4451 which relates to the vendor rating on GreenMarine Ltd. Insights shows all three on one screen.

A BI tool bolted on top of a fragmented data landscape can only surface trends within its silo. VOLAX Insights sits on the unified database beneath all sixteen modules — so cause and effect across departments become visible in ways siloed BI never can.

Ten Capabilities

Fleet intelligence at every level

Configurable dashboards

Dashboards per role (CFO, fleet manager, superintendent, master, crewing) with drag-and-drop widget composition. Drill from fleet KPI to individual record without leaving the dashboard.

Fleet-wide KPI monitoring

Maintenance PPE (Percentage Planned Executed), procurement lead time, safety recordable-rate, crew rest-hour compliance %, CII attained. All KPIs surface fleet-wide and per-vessel.

Automated report generation

Daily, weekly, monthly reports scheduled and delivered to email lists as PDF. Board pack, monthly review, quarterly review, annual sustainability report — all templated, all automated.

Cross-module correlation

Root cause analysis across modules: a PMS overdue trend correlated with a specific spare delivery delay correlated with a specific vendor. Trend charts drawn from the same base data.

Vessel benchmarking

Compare cost, performance, and compliance across sister vessels and across the whole fleet. Outlier identification. Best-in-class analysis with attribution.

Budget monitoring

Forecast-vs-actual visualisation per cost centre and vessel. Rolling forecast with confidence bands. Overrun alerts routed to owners.

Safety scorecards

Leading and lagging safety indicators combined into a scorecard. Trending recordable rates. Near-miss reporting rate as a leading indicator. Fleet SHEQ league table.

Operational efficiency metrics

Vessel availability, in-service days, off-hire days, downtime by cause, dry-dock cycle time, on-schedule departure %. The metrics that matter to charterers and owners.

Export to PowerPoint / PDF / Excel

Board-ready exports one click away. Native PowerPoint slide deck, styled PDF, or Excel workbook with underlying data. Consistent branding.

Role-based access

Each stakeholder sees only their relevant metrics. Executive suite view. Superintendent view. Master view. Crewing view. All from the same underlying dataset.
