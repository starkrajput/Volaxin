A dry dock is a two-week concentrate of everything your fleet does in five years.

Work collected over a survey cycle, spares procured months in advance, contractors coordinated across time zones, class surveyors booked, safety permits generated hourly, and every hour of yard time carrying five-digit cost. Ten Capabilities

From defect list to warranty period, one platform

Work collection from PMS defects & surveys

Every open defect, deferred maintenance item, and upcoming class survey pulled into the docking specification. Superintendent reviews, prioritises, approves — then the spec is complete.

Specification template library

Templates organised by vessel type, scope, and yard. Sister-vessel spec re-use. Standard items pre-priced from historical actuals.

Competitive yard tendering

RFQ distribution to shortlisted yards. Structured quotation return. Side-by-side price comparison per work item. Award audit trail.

Real-time budget monitoring

Approved estimate baseline. Actuals captured as the docking runs. Variance analysis by scope item, contractor, and category. Superintendent-approved variations tracked with root cause.

Project timeline management

Docking Gantt with critical path. Milestone tracking (arrival, dry-out, undocking, sea trial, redelivery). Class attendance and survey completion visible in the same timeline.

VOLAX Procure integration

Owner-supplied spares and services ordered from the same platform. Delivery to yard tracked. Yard-supplied variations reconciled against the yard quote.

Historical docking knowledge base

Every past docking preserved: spec, contractors, actuals, variations, defects found in situ, lessons for next dock. Sister-vessel comparison for future planning.

Steel weight & surface area calculations

Hull renewal steel weight, surface area for coating, cathodic protection area. Auto-calculated from inspection findings in VOLAX Hull for accurate yard tendering.

Photo documentation

Before / during / after photos tied to spec items. Contractor progress evidence. Warranty dispute support with time-stamped image trail.

Post-docking warranty tracking

Warranty period per contractor per work item. Auto-alerts on defect discovery within warranty window — with claim documentation package generated for the yard.
