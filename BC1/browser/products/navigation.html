Why the office and the bridge should see the same map

When your operations superintendent and your master argue about a port ETA, one of them has the wrong data.

In most fleets, the bridge runs ECDIS and voyage planning tools, the office runs a separate AIS tracking system, and noon reports flow through a third tool. Three datasets, three versions of the truth. VOLAX Navigate collapses them into one — the position the master sees is the position the office sees is the position the charterer’s laytime clock uses.

Ten Capabilities

Navigation as a first-class ERP module

AIS vessel tracking

Real-time position, course, speed over ground, and heading. Historical trajectory with speed profile. Live alerts on deviation from planned route.

Chart-based route planning

Waypoint management on interactive nautical charts. Distance and rhumb-line calculations. Alternative route comparison. Voyage stitching for multi-leg trades.

Weather integration

Wind, current, significant wave height, swell period, and pressure overlays. Multi-model comparison. Fleet-view weather threats highlighted at a glance.

Geofenced compliance alerts

Emission Control Areas (SECA/NECA), Traffic Separation Schemes, marine protected areas, war-risk zones. Real-time alerts on entry with fuel-switching prompts.

Port information database

Berth details, draught restrictions, tide windows, agent contacts, regulatory requirements, PSC focus themes, pilotage rules. Historical port-call data per vessel.

SOLAS-aligned passage planning

Berth-to-berth passage plans with the four SOLAS stages (appraisal, planning, execution, monitoring). Master approval workflow. PSC-ready documentation.

Voyage progress monitoring

ETA rolled forward against current speed and weather. CP speed compliance monitored. Deviation alerts. Charterer-facing progress reports auto-generated.

Automated noon report feed

Position, distance, speed, weather, and fuel captured at noon — automatically populated from AIS and sensors. Master reviews and files. No re-typing.

Fleet movement world map

Every vessel on one interactive globe. Filter by trade, charterer, cargo, or SHEQ status. Click any vessel for full context. Presentation-ready for board meetings.

CII-aware route optimization

Route options ranked on ETA, fuel, and attained CII impact. Choose the passage that preserves your carbon rating.
