Why document control at sea is different

Your fleet is looking at 12 different revisions of the same SMS procedure right now.

Without proper version control, an SMS update issued from the office takes weeks to permeate the fleet. Meanwhile, PSC inspectors are checking the current revision, class surveyors are auditing procedure adherence, and your third officer is following last year’s checklist. VOLAX Docs collapses that lag to hours — and proves the current revision was acknowledged, per rank, per vessel.

Ten Capabilities

Document control fit for auditors and seafarers

Version control with full history

Every document carries revision history from first publication. Superseded revisions preserved for audit. Change summary per revision. Author, reviewer, approver captured.

Mandatory reading with acknowledgment

Assign a document as mandatory for named ranks. Track acknowledgment per crew member. Compliance report per vessel per revision. PSC-defensible evidence trail.

Smart interlinking

Procedures reference other procedures, checklists, forms, and drawings. Interlinks preserved across revisions. Broken links flagged before publication.

Full-text search with filters

Every document indexed and searchable. Filters by type, department, vessel, date, and revision status. Find the checklist the second engineer needs in seconds.

Fleet-wide differential distribution

Only the changed bytes travel over satellite. A 200-page manual with one paragraph change syncs in kilobytes, not megabytes.

Access control per document

Sensitive documents (personnel files, commercial contracts, security plans) restricted per role, rank, and vessel. Audit trail on every read.

Digital form filling & signatures

Forms and checklists filled inside the system. Digital signatures with role and timestamp. No print, sign, scan, re-upload.

Native format support

PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, CAD drawings, video, HTML procedures. All searchable, all version-controlled, all offline-available.

VOLAX SHEQ integration

ISM procedures managed in Docs, referenced from SHEQ risk assessments, permits, and audits. Change one, update all references.

Offline availability, auto-sync

Every vessel carries a complete document library locally. Updates sync when connectivity resumes — differentially, compressed, encrypted.
