Why crew software should feel maritime

A seafarer’s life spans a certificate matrix, a CBA schedule, a rotation cycle, and a family. Generic HR software sees none of it.

Managing a ship’s crew is not managing employees. It is orchestrating people across three or four time zones, ten or twelve nationalities, twenty overlapping regulatory regimes, and hundreds of certificate expiries — while paying them in the currency their family bank accepts. VOLAX Crew is engineered around that reality; nothing about it feels retrofitted from land HR.

Ten Capabilities

Purpose-built for maritime workforce management

Visual crew planner

Drag-and-drop crew assignment across a fleet-wide timeline. Automatic rotation proposals honouring rank matrix, CBA cycle, nationality mix rules, and endorsement requirements. Conflict detection before you commit.

Maritime payroll engine

Multi-currency processing, wage scales per rank per flag, seniority increments, overtime rates, victualling deductions, bond, allotments, CBA-driven benefits. Payslips in the seafarer’s language, credited to the seafarer’s bank.

MLC 2006, STCW, ILO 180, OPA 90

Work-rest hour compliance under every relevant regime. Automatic recording where sensors permit, keyed input where they don’t, master review before submission.

Rolling-window violation detection

The critical STCW compliance step most systems miss: hidden violations spanning day boundaries. VOLAX Crew evaluates every rolling 24-hour and 7-day window, catches the non-conformity, and prompts the master before PSC does.

Certificate & license tracking

Every seafarer’s STCW certificates, endorsements, medicals, and flag documentation held in one dossier. Automatic expiry alerts at 90 / 60 / 30 / 7 days — to the crewing office and to the seafarer.

Training records & competence matrix

Rank competence requirements defined at fleet level. Individual training records checked against the matrix. Gap analysis surfaces upcoming shortfalls before they become deployment blockers.

Crew travel coordination

Sign-on and sign-off travel bookings tracked through the crewing agent. Cost captured in real time, per vessel, per voyage. Delayed-flight impact on rotation surfaced automatically.

Self-service crew portal

Seafarers submit documents, view schedules, request leave, and download payslips through a portal or mobile app — in their language. Reduces crewing-office load; improves seafarer satisfaction.

Performance appraisal

Structured appraisal at end of tour, promotion consideration, disciplinary history, feedback loop into recruitment. Historical records preserved for the length of the career.

Master’s cash & General Account

Onboard petty cash, ship-side crew expenses, disbursements, and the master’s General Account reconciled onshore. No end-of-voyage envelope-of-receipts.
